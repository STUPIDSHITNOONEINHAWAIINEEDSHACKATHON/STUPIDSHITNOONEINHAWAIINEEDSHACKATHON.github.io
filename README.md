# Stupid Shit No One In Hawaii Needs and Terrible Ideas Hackathon

The site for a one-day hackathon in Honolulu in January 2017, for projects with no value whatsoever. Ray Farias and Joe Karlsson put it on. It's still up at [stupidshitnooneinhawaiineedshackathon.github.io](https://stupidshitnooneinhawaiineedshackathon.github.io/), with every entry, the winners and the names of everyone who built them.

## Artist statement

In 2016 Ray and I went to a new hackathon about once or twice a month, and most of them were sponsored by a company that wanted something back: free development work, developers using its product, or new hires. Ray Farias and I put on the Stupid Shit No One In Hawaii Needs and Terrible Ideas Hackathon to do the opposite. Everyone there was building for the joy of making something, not for profit or glory.
